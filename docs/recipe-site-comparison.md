# What helps someone choose dinner?

Reviewed November 26, 2026. The comparison starts with all five sites on Similarweb’s public US cooking and recipes list for October 2026, followed by specialist references. Traffic estimates establish the sample. The product observations below come from the sites. This is a review of interfaces, not a claim about reader preference or cooking outcomes. [Similarweb ranking and month](https://www.similarweb.com/top-websites/united-states/food-and-drink/cooking-and-recipes/)

| Site | What a reader can do | Decision for Open Food Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/) | Search for a recipe or ingredient and read contributions from home cooks. | Start the cookbook with ingredient search and give home cooks a way to credit and submit their version. |
| [Delish](https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/) | Browse dinner ideas grouped around familiar meal decisions. | Let someone reach dishes directly from the homepage. |
| [The Pioneer Woman](https://www.thepioneerwoman.com/food-cooking/recipes/) | Enter through easy recipes or dinner collections. | Put the time commitment on the card, including prerequisites like having cooked rice. |
| [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes) | Browse by ingredients or constraints such as 30-minute dinners. | Combine required ingredients, style and a maximum time in one search. |
| [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/recipes) | Explore dinner ideas, popular ingredients and a searchable recipe collection. | Connect an ingredient result to an actionable dish and that day’s planner. |
| [Budget Bytes](https://www.budgetbytes.com/quick-seasoned-black-beans/) | See cost, step photos and serving information for a specific recipe. | Show servings and explain the step that changes the texture. Leave cost estimates to contributors with actual prices and units. |
| [King Arthur Baking](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-sandwich-bread-recipe) | Check timings and yield, then use physical cues such as dough texture to decide when a step is done. | Describe observable cues for doneness, especially in addition to clock times. |
| [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/recipes/) | Browse recipes through ingredient and meal groupings. | Allow several ingredients in one query. Offer food composition lookup when there is no recipe match. |

## What the guide adds

From the sesame tofu bowl, a reader can learn how to get a golden crust, find a substitution, compare protein in two USDA tofu records and put the bowl on a particular day. The source note identifies the food preparation and units. For a drink linked to a trial, it identifies what the participants drank and whether the home version has been tested.

## A check for the next round

Give a new reader this task: “You have tofu, cucumber and cooked rice. Find dinner in 20 minutes, learn why firm tofu entries differ, and save the meal for Friday.” Observe whether they can finish without help and where they pause. A functioning search and planner establish technical feasibility. Actual reader sessions are needed to judge whether the design makes dinner easier.

## Pantry tools and food education

Reviewed November 26, 2026. The goal is for a cook to find a meal using the food they have, understand one ingredient and put it on their calendar. These observations cover the linked public pages and instructions.

| Resource | What the public page shows | What to check in Open Food Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

[Ingredient finder](https://www.pantryformula.com/) | Can someone tell which staple is missing, check the amount in the full recipe and save the meal to a particular day? |

## A pantry check volunteers can run

**Volunteer results are unmeasured.** The following targets are proposed for a first round with five first-time readers and two independent home cooks. Count completed sessions and record where someone needed help. These small samples can reveal specific problems. They cannot establish a site ranking.

1. **Find and plan lunch.** Start in a fresh browser session. Give the reader a scenario. “You have plain canned light tuna in water and canned black olives. Find a desk lunch where USDA lists 0 g total sugars. Which version can you make now? What would you buy for the other version? Pick one and save it for Friday lunch.” Start the timer when they open the [pantry picker](https://bennewell35.github.io/open-food-source/#pantry-picker). Stop when they refresh and show the same version on Friday. The target is for four of five to finish unaided in three minutes, including identifying olive oil for the original version.
2. **Explain the food evidence.** Ask the same reader why the tuna dish qualifies and whether peanut butter oats can have no added sugar while still containing sugar. Ask them to show one linked source. The target is for four of five to locate the source and distinguish the reference food’s total sugars from sugars added during preparation. Record their explanation in their own words.
3. **Make a meal.** Ask two volunteers to prepare a pantry recipe they want to eat, using the amount stated and their usual equipment. Record actual time from opening the first ingredient to serving, yield, and the first step they had to interpret. The target is for both cooks to serve the stated yield without an unlisted ingredient or tool, within five minutes of the published time.
4. **Come back to cook.** Invite the five readers to use the site again the following week if they find it useful. Count how many report actually making or planning a second meal, and ask which feature brought them back. Two of five returning voluntarily is the initial target. Record unanswered invitations separately.

If a target is missed, revise the step where cooks first got stuck and rerun it with new readers. To contribute a result, title a community recipe issue “Pantry check — [dish name]”. Include which task you tried, what happened, the elapsed time and one wording or recipe change you would make.
